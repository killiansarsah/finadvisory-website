# FinAdvisory Hero Video Directory

Place your generated hero background video here with the name:
`hero-background.mp4`

### Recommended Video Specs:
- **Format**: MP4 (H.264 video codec, AAC audio or no audio stream)
- **Resolution**: 1920x1080 (1080p) or 2560x1440 (1440p)
- **Framerate**: 24fps - 30fps
- **Bitrate**: 3 - 6 Mbps (optimized for web loading)
- **Duration**: 5 - 10 seconds seamless loop
